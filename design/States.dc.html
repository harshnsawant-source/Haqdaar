Indigo is deliberately not red. A refusal is the system working correctly. Painting it as an error teaches the room to read honesty as breakage.

Rules the UI must not break

  1. The UI never writes a sentence about a verdict. Every line arrives from the engine in lines, approval_lines, banners, window_lines. Render them in that order. Composing your own copy here breaks the guarantee the whole product rests on.
  2. The approval block never merges into eligibility. Own border, own colour, always below. "You are entitled to apply" and "they will say yes" are different claims.
  3. A closed scheme leads with the closure, greys its body, and shows no action button. The engine also returns 409 if you call the action endpoint anyway.
  4. The SIMULATED banner comes before any filled field and is never dismissible.
  5. Never sort or score. There is no "best match" and no percentage. The order the engine returns is the order you render.
  6. Every card shows when it was checked and links its government source.
