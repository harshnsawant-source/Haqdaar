WHAT CHANGED FROM THE OLD OUTLINE, AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The previous version was written before the fused SIH26092 decision and before
the corpus was verified. Five things changed:

1. The ENTREPRENEUR now leads. Sunita moves to the reveal.
2. Slide 2's statistic is gone. It was a disability-aid survey number that fits a
   welfare pitch, not this one. Replaced with a finding of our own that we can
   prove live on stage, which is stronger than any borrowed statistic.
3. NEW SLIDE 8, the closed door. This is the best material in the deck and it did
   not exist before, because the finding did not exist before.
4. The reveal is welfare AND student, not the legal vertical that was never built.
5. Slide 9's action beat files into NSFDC, not Stand-Up India, because Stand-Up
   India is one of the two schemes that turned out to be closed.

APPENDIX - NOT PRESENTED. FOR Q&A ONLY.
================================================================================
Build these. They are how you answer a hard question in ten seconds instead of
ninety.

A1  ARCHITECTURE
    corpus -> evaluator -> Guard -> deterministic renderer -> API -> UI.
    ZERO generative model calls anywhere in the pipeline. No API keys. The test
    suite passes with every key stripped from the environment.
    Eligibility is decided by code, never by a model.

A2  THE CORPUS, ALL TEN, WITH LIVE LINKS
    entrepreneur   stand-up-india (CLOSED), nsfdc-term-loan, vcf-sc (CLOSED)
    welfare        pm-kisan, ignwps, sgnay, pmjay, avvc
    student        pre-matric-sc, top-class-sc
    9 of 10 transcribed verbatim from official sources, each with its URL and the
    date we read it. Answers "is your corpus real" by letting them click one.

A3  THE GUARD'S SIX TRIGGERS
    T1 unsupported predicate   T2 missing but obtainable   T3 no retrieval support
    T4 citation integrity      T5 stale rule               T6 lapsed scheme
    351 tests, all green, no network needed.

A4  WHAT WE DID NOT BUILD, AND WHY
    No login. We hold caste, income and widowhood. Behind an account that becomes
    a breach target for the most vulnerable people in the country. Everything
    stays on her device. She never signs up and it works offline.
    Also out: live portal filing, admin panel, voice, all schemes.
    Owning your scope is a strength when YOU raise it.

A5  THE STACKING RULE
    In Maharashtra a widow's Rs 1,500 is Rs 300 central plus Rs 1,200 state, not
    two separate Rs 1,500 benefits. The state's own page says so. We group them so
    nothing is ever double-counted. This slide impresses anyone who actually knows
    welfare administration.
